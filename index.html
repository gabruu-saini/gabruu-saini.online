✦ My Story

Computer Science is the unifying thread behind everything I create.

I am Ashween Singh Saini, based in Hoshiarpur, Punjab. For me, Computer Science has never been just code syntax or exam marks—it is the study of how information moves, how complexity behaves under constraint, and how deterministic logic creates resilient systems in an unpredictable world.

From First Principles to Clean Algorithms

My journey began with deep immersion in ISC Class 12 Computer Science theory: mastering recursion trees, multidimensional matrix transformations, composite object relationships, and the mathematics of algorithm complexity. I built the 53-program ISC CS Compendium not just as a reference, but as an interactive sandbox to prove that even classical board curriculum can be rendered with modern engineering craft.

Understanding recursion at the stack-frame level and tracing variables through dry-run tables taught me discipline: code is meant to be verified, predictable, and clean.

Systems, Concurrency & Defensive Mindset

That discipline led me naturally to Rust and backend systems. Moving away from garbage collection into Rust's ownership model reshaped my thinking about resource management, thread safety, and zero-cost abstractions. Concurrently, studying for CompTIA Network+, Cisco CCNA, and CompTIA PenTest+ gave me an appreciation for the wire: how packets traverse switches, how TLS handshakes defend data in flight, and where systems fail when assumptions break.

When you know how a network packet is assembled from Layer 2 to Layer 7, you become both a better systems engineer and a sharper security researcher.
